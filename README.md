# My Technology Portfolio

## Milestone 2: Accessibility and Visual Design

### Accessbility Improvements 

For this assignment, I used the WAVE accessibility checker to test my Home, Projects, and About pages. 
When I first tested my website, WAVE found one HTML language error and three color contast errors. 
I fixed the language error by correcting my HTML code to include 'lang="eng"'. this helps screen readers recognize the language of my website. 
I also chnaged my pink text to a darker shade because the original color did not have enough contrast against the gray background. This makes my website easier to read for people with vision difficulties.
I added a contact form to my About page with properly connected labels, required fields, and error messages to help users correct mistakes. 
After making these changes, I tested my website again using WAVE. All three pages received a score 10 out of 10 with zero reported errors. 

### Gestalt Design Principles 

For my Technology Portfolio website, I used two Gesalt design principles: proximity and similarity. 

**1. Proximity**

I used proximity by keeping related information together on my website. For example, on my homepage, I grouped my Technology and My Hobbies information into separate cards. I also kept my headings close to the information they describe. This makes my website eaiser to read and helps visitors understand which informatin belongs together. 

**2. Similarity**

I used similarity by keeping the same colors, fonts, and design styles throughout my website. All three pages use my pink, white, and gray color scheme along with my Cutie Pop font. I also used matching card designs, borders, and navigation menus. This helps my website look organized and makes easier for visiotrs to recognize similar sections.
